Screen Time Authorization

One Task needs Apple Screen Time authorization to block selected apps, websites, or categories. If authorization was denied or revoked, return to Choose distractions, confirm Screen Time access, and start blocking again. Apple controls whether the authorization prompt appears again.

Blocking Did Not Start

One Task reports when blocking cannot be confirmed. Keep the assignment and selected distractions saved, then try starting blocking again after confirming Screen Time access.

Website Coverage

Website blocking depends on Apple's Screen Time coverage and can vary by browser, website, and device state. One Task does not claim reliable coverage for every browser or website.

Ending Blocking Early

From the active assignment, choose the early-ending option and follow the Standard or Strong confirmation. Selected apps, websites, and categories reopen, and the local task record notes that blocking ended early.

Purchases And Restore

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Use Restore Purchases on the One Task paywall when an active subscription is not recognized. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings.
